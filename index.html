iLanis App Factory · idea #16

Four tiles today.
Fifteen soon.

A pattern of tiles lights up for a second, then vanishes. Click it back from memory. Clear a level and the pattern grows; the grid itself gets bigger as you climb. Three wrong tiles end the run.

Test your memory
🧠

Real memory training

Spatial working memory is one of the most trainable brain skills. This is the classic protocol, distilled to a game.

📈

It keeps growing

3x3 becomes 4x4 becomes 5x5. Four tiles becomes fifteen. The ceiling is your brain, not the app.

❤

Three lives

Miss a tile and the pattern shows itself one more time. Miss three and the run ends - but your best level is kept.

⚡

No setup

No account, no daily quest, no coins. Press start, stare at tiles, feel neurons strain.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
